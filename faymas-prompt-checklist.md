# Faymas Prompt Checklist

## 1. Couple portrait
- What it is: A romantic or natural portrait of two people.
- Best for: Beach scenes, indoor portraits, wedding-style images, or casual couple photos.
- How to change it: Replace the relationship, clothing, location, pose, and emotional tone.

## 2. Family portrait
- What it is: A prompt adapted for parents, children, or siblings.
- Best for: Personal memory images and family-focused edits.
- How to change it: State the exact relationship, age difference, skin tone, facial features, and natural expression.

## 3. Cinematic portrait
- What it is: A portrait using dramatic light, framing, and atmosphere.
- Best for: Posters, profile images, fashion edits, and story-driven visuals.
- How to change it: Adjust the camera angle, light direction, background, color mood, and clothing.

## 4. Realistic photo edit
- What it is: A prompt designed to preserve a person while changing the scene or styling.
- Best for: Photo transformations that should still look believable.
- How to change it: Add “preserve facial identity,” then specify what may change and what must stay the same.

## 5. Father-and-child image
- What it is: A relationship-focused family prompt.
- Best for: Creating a respectful portrait of a parent and child.
- How to change it: Specify the relationship, age, pose, distance between subjects, clothing, and setting.

## 6. Social-media portrait
- What it is: A visually strong portrait intended for sharing.
- Best for: Profile images, short-video thumbnails, and social posts.
- How to change it: Adapt the framing, aspect ratio, background contrast, and amount of visual detail.
